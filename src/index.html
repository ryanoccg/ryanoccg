Client Project Full-Stack 2 months

Uwrapz — Car Wrapping Workflow Platform

Solo developer · 0 → production · Frontend + Backend + Integrations

The Challenge

Car wrapping shops manually tracked vehicle progress on paper and over phone calls — leaving customers anxious and staff overloaded with status enquiries. The team needed a single source of truth their customers could check anytime.

What I Built

An end-to-end web platform: a Laravel admin dashboard for staff to log wrap stages, a customer-facing status page, and a WhatsApp Business API integration that auto-notifies customers when their car moves to the next stage — no manual messages required.

Outcome

Replaced the team's ad-hoc SOP with a streamlined digital workflow. Customers stopped calling for updates because they got real-time WhatsApp notifications. Client reported smoother daily operations and a more professional brand experience.

# How Much Does a Website Cost in Malaysia? (2026 Guide)

**Quick answer:** A professional website in Malaysia costs between **RM 1,500 and RM 15,000+**, depending on complexity. Here's exactly what you get at each price range.

If you're a business owner in Malaysia looking to build a website, this guide breaks down the real costs — no vague "it depends" answers.

## What's Included in Each Package?

### Simple Business Website (RM 1,500 – 3,000)

This is the most common request from Malaysian SMEs. You get:

- 5 static pages (Home, About, Services, Portfolio, Contact)
- Mobile-responsive design
- Basic SEO setup
- Contact form with WhatsApp integration
- Google Analytics installation
- Delivery in 1-2 weeks

**Best for:** Restaurants, hair salons, dental clinics, tuition centres, and service businesses in Penang, KL, and other major cities.

![Business website on mobile](UNSPLASH:1)

### CMS Website with Blog (RM 3,500 – 6,000)

When you need to update content regularly without calling your developer:

- Everything in the Simple package
- WordPress or custom CMS backend
- Blog functionality (SEO-optimized)
- Multi-language support (optional)
- Lead capture forms
- 30 days of post-launch support

**Best for:** Businesses that want to rank on Google through content marketing.

### Custom Web Application (RM 8,000 – 25,000+)

For businesses with unique workflows that off-the-shelf solutions can't handle:

- Custom-built with Laravel, Vue.js, or similar frameworks
- User authentication and role management
- Admin dashboard
- API integrations (payment gateways, WhatsApp, etc.)
- Database architecture
- Ongoing maintenance options

**Best for:** Booking platforms, inventory systems, customer portals, and internal tools.

## Hidden Costs You Should Know

Don't just look at the development price. Budget for these annual costs:

| Item | Annual Cost (MYR) |
|------|-------------------|
| Domain (.com) | RM 50 – 80 |
| Shared Hosting | RM 200 – 500 |
| VPS/Cloud Hosting | RM 600 – 2,400 |
| SSL Certificate | RM 0 (free with Let's Encrypt) |
| Email Hosting | RM 0 – 300 |
| Maintenance / Updates | RM 0 – 3,600 |

**Pro tip:** Many developers include the first year of hosting in their quote. Always ask what happens in Year 2.

## Red Flags When Hiring a Developer

Watch out for these warning signs:

- **Price is too low** (below RM 800 for a "custom" website) — likely using templates with minimal customization
- **No portfolio** — you can't see their past work
- **100% upfront payment** — industry standard is 50/50
- **No contract** — always get a written agreement
- **Can't explain their tech stack** — they should know what they're building with

## How to Choose the Right Developer

Ask these questions before signing:

1. Can I see 3 similar projects you've completed?
2. What happens if I want changes after launch?
3. Who owns the source code?
4. How do I update content myself?
5. What's your response time for bug fixes?

## FAQ

### How long does it take to build a website in Malaysia?
Simple sites take 1-2 weeks. CMS websites take 2-4 weeks. Custom applications take 2-6 months depending on features.

### Can I build a website myself for cheaper?
Yes, using Wix or Squarespace (RM 50-200/month). But you trade customization, speed, and SEO potential. For serious businesses, hiring a developer is worth it.

### Do I need to pay monthly after the website is built?
Only for hosting (RM 15-200/month) and optional maintenance. The website itself is yours — you own the code.

### Should I use WordPress or a custom-built site?
WordPress is great for blogs and content sites. Custom-built (Laravel/Vue) is better for unique features and complex systems. [See my full comparison here](#).

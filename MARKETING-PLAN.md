### Google Ads (Budget: RM 300-500/month)

**Strategy:** Only bid on high buyer-intent keywords

| Keyword | Est. CPC | Conversion Potential |
|---------|----------|---------------------|
| "hire web developer penang" | RM 2-4 | High ⭐⭐⭐ |
| "website developer malaysia" | RM 1.5-3 | Medium ⭐⭐ |

## 🚨 Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. ❌ Keyword stuffing — Write naturally, don't force keywords
2. ❌ Buying backlinks — Google penalizes this
3. ❌ Duplicate content — Each blog post must be unique
4. ❌ Ignoring mobile — 70% of Malaysia traffic is mobile
5. ❌ No internal linking — Link blog posts to #pricing
6. ❌ Thin content — Each article should be 1,000+ words
7. ❌ Expecting overnight results — SEO takes 3-6 months
